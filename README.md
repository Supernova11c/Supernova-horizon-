Supernova Horizon License

Version 0.1.0

The Supernova Horizon License is a proposed permissive software license developed by Supernova. It explores ways to clarify software redistribution, security-fix information, patent permissions, and the boundaries between software and AI-related materials.

Goals

The project aims to develop a clear, practical license that:

- Permits personal, academic, commercial, and other lawful uses of covered software.
- Allows modification, redistribution, and integration into proprietary products.
- Clarifies the treatment of existing security-fix references and related metadata during redistribution.
- Defines how separately licensed dependencies, AI models, datasets, and generated materials are treated.
- Provides express copyright and patent permissions.
- Establishes understandable rules for attribution, termination, warranties, and liability.

Current Status

The license is published for discussion, feedback, and further legal and technical review. It has not been approved by the Open Source Initiative (OSI).

Publication of this proposal does not establish that it is legally validated, compatible with every other license, or suitable for every project.

Design Principles

1. Permissive redistribution

The proposal allows covered software to be used, modified, redistributed, and incorporated into proprietary products, subject to its terms.

2. Security-information continuity

The proposal explores preserving relevant security-fix information already supplied with a covered Work, without requiring distributors to discover vulnerabilities, publish fixes, or maintain software.

3. Clear AI-component boundaries

The proposal distinguishes software from separately licensed AI models, model parameters, datasets, outputs, and external services. Coverage depends on the license grant and applicable rights; it is not automatic.

4. Predictable legal terms

The proposal includes provisions addressing patent permissions, attribution, termination, warranties, and liability.

Contributing and Feedback

Feedback is welcome on clarity, usability, legal drafting, compatibility with existing licenses, and whether the proposed clauses solve problems that existing licenses do not adequately address.

Please explain the issue, provide a concrete example where possible, and suggest a practical improvement.

Open Source Initiative Review

The project may seek review by the Open Source Initiative after its rationale, legal wording, and comparison with existing licenses have been developed sufficiently.

OSI review is a separate process. No approval is implied by this repository's publication.

Stewardship

The Supernova project maintains this proposal and its version history. The project may publish revisions following review and discussion.
